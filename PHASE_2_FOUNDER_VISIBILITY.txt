STEP 1: GET A PROFESSIONAL HEADSHOT (Today or Tomorrow)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT YOU NEED:
  - Professional photo: chest-up, neutral background, looking at camera
  - Head size: ~40% of frame
  - Expression: friendly but serious (approachable, not goofy)
  - Background: clean, white or light gray (no pattern)
  - Lighting: good (no harsh shadows on face)
  - Format: JPG or PNG, 300x300px minimum

WHERE TO GET IT:

Option 1: DIY (Fast, Free)
  - Phone with good camera
  - Wear solid blue or gray shirt
  - Go outside or near window (natural light)
  - Take 50+ shots from different angles
  - Pick best one
  - Crop to headshot
  - Upload to PhotoShop or Pixlr for quick cleanup

Option 2: Professional (Best, $50-200)
  - Go to local portrait photographer
  - Book 30-min session
  - Tell them: "Corporate headshot for SaaS founder"
  - Get digital files
  - Done

Option 3: Professional Online (Good, $25-100)
  - Fiverr: search "professional headshot"
  - Upwork: find a photographer
  - They send you clean digital file

TIMELINE: Today if you go DIY. 1-2 weeks if you book professional.

For now: Use your best LinkedIn photo cropped as square.


STEP 2: WRITE YOUR FOUNDER STORY (Today - 30 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This is critical. Your story is why they trust you.

FORMULA (150 words):
  1. Your problem (what you experienced)
  2. The discovery (why it mattered)
  3. The insight (how you realized the solution)
  4. What you built (how you fixed it)
  5. Why you care (what success looks like for customers)

EXAMPLE TEMPLATE (replace with your story):

"I'm [Your Name], founder of JARVIS PRIME.

For three years, I watched marketing agencies get crushed by lead generation 
costs. They'd spend $4,000-$8,000/month hiring SDRs who needed 2-3 months to 
ramp. Half the time, it didn't work.

One night, I realized: agencies don't need more tools. They need results.

I started manually sending cold emails on behalf of agencies — finding prospects, 
writing personal emails, following up, booking meetings. Within 60 days, I'd 
booked [X] qualified meetings for my first client.

That's when JARVIS PRIME was born.

Now, I help agencies get 8-15 qualified meetings/month without the hiring 
headaches. Simple process. Honest metrics. Money-back guarantee if we don't 
deliver.

If you're an agency struggling to fill your pipeline, let's talk."


YOUR STORY (FILL IN):

[Your problem: What was broken?]


[Your discovery: What changed?]


[Your insight: How did you solve it?]


[What you built: How is it different?]


[Why you care: What does success look like?]


TIPS:
  ✓ Be specific about numbers (not "many agencies" but "agencies spending $4-8K")
  ✓ Be honest about struggles (not "I was always great at sales" but "I realized")
  ✓ Show you've built this before (mention relevant experience)
  ✓ Make it about them (not "I'm awesome" but "agencies are struggling")
  ✓ End with action (let's talk, book a call)
