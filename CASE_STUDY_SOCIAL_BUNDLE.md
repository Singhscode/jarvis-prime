### PRESS RELEASE: Crescendo Ventures Triples Pipeline Growth with AI-Powered Sales Automation

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Bangalore, India** – Crescendo Ventures, a B2B SaaS agency focused on sales enablement and account-based marketing, today announced that it has achieved a 3x increase in qualified pipeline within 90 days using JARVIS PRIME's AI-powered sales automation platform.

**The Challenge**
Crescendo Ventures operates a team of 8 SDRs generating 3–5 qualified meetings per month despite significant investment. The company faced a common growth ceiling: scale required hiring, but hiring carried high costs and uncertain ROI.

**The Solution**
Rather than expand headcount, Crescendo implemented JARVIS PRIME's automation platform to:
- Score and prioritize 2,500+ prospects using ICP
- Generate personalized outreach at scale (3,200 emails/month)
- Automate lead routing and qualification
- Optimize sequences based on real-time performance data

**The Results**
In 90 days:
- Qualified leads increased from 8–12/month to 35–40/month (+290%)
- Meetings booked increased from 3–5/month to 11–13/month (+260%)
- New MRR added: ₹1.2L–₹1.5L
- Cost per lead reduced by 70%
- Payback period: 5 months

Additionally, team satisfaction improved from 5.2/10 to 8.1/10, and annual turnover decreased from 40% to 12%.

**Quote from Leadership**
"We thought we needed to hire more SDRs," said Priya Sharma, CEO of Crescendo Ventures. "Instead, we automated the process and freed our existing team to close deals. Eight SDRs plus JARVIS is better than 15 SDRs doing it manually."

**About Crescendo Ventures**
Crescendo Ventures is a B2B SaaS agency helping mid-market companies build scalable sales motions. Operating since 2019, the company serves 12–15 clients simultaneously with contract values ranging from ₹12L–₹60L annually.

**About JARVIS PRIME**
JARVIS PRIME is an AI-powered sales automation platform designed for B2B companies and agencies. The platform helps businesses generate qualified pipeline, scale outreach without hiring, and close more deals—with zero additional headcount required.
