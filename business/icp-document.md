# JARVIS PRIME — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Primary ICP: B2B Agency Owner (India)

### Demographics
- **Role:** Founder / Co-Founder / MD
- **Company size:** 5–50 employees
- **Age:** 25–42
- **Location:** Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune
- **Revenue:** ₹10L–₹2Cr/month

### Company Profile
- **Industry:** Digital marketing agency, performance agency, SEO agency, SaaS startup, IT services, staffing/recruitment
- **Tech stack:** Uses tools like HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Notion, Slack, Freshsales
- **Current outbound:** Has 1–3 SDRs OR is doing manual LinkedIn/email outreach
- **Pain level:** High — lead generation is their #1 growth blocker

### Psychographics
- Thinks about growth daily
- Has heard of AI automation but hasn't implemented it
- Trusts ROI-based decisions — wants proof before buying
- Responds to case studies, demos, and concrete numbers
- Active on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Twitter/X

### Trigger Events (when to reach out)
- Recently hired sales/BD roles (LinkedIn posts)
- Posted about lead gen struggles
- Running ads but no organic outbound
- Just raised funding or announced expansion
- Hiring SDRs (Naukri/LinkedIn job posts — means they need outbound)

### Qualifying Questions (BANT)
1. **Budget:** "Do you currently spend on sales tools or SDRs?" (₹15K+/mo = qualified)
2. **Authority:** Are they the decision maker? (Founder/MD/VP Sales)
3. **Need:** Are they actively doing outbound? Any targets for meetings/month?
4. **Timeline:** "When would you want a system like this live?" (<30 days = hot)

## Secondary ICP: SaaS Startup Founder (India)

### Profile
- Early-stage SaaS (0–50 customers), trying to find product-market fit
- Needs meetings with decision-makers but has no sales team
- Budget: ₹20K–₹50K/mo for growth
- Platforms: ProductHunt, IndieHackers, LinkedIn, Twitter

### Outreach approach
- Lead with "free pilot week" offer
- Reference their product/latest feature in opening line
- Focus on: "Book 5–10 demo calls/month guaranteed in 30 days"

---

## ICP Scoring Matrix (1–5 per dimension, 20+ = hot lead)

| Dimension | Weight | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Budget signals | 5 | Hiring SDR, running paid ads, team > 10 |
| Pain signals | 5 | Post about lead gen, SDR issues, growth goals |
| Authority | 4 | Founder / C-suite title |
| Market fit | 4 | B2B product/service, ticket > ₹10K |
| Timing | 2 | Recent funding, expansion, hiring |

---

## What They Are NOT (Exclusion Criteria)
- B2C companies
- D2C / e-commerce brands
- Companies < ₹5L/month revenue
- Companies with no existing product/service to sell
- Freelancers / solopreneurs (unless strong revenue signal)
