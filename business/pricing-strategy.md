# JARVIS PRIME — Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Ladder

| Tier | Setup Fee | Monthly Retainer | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹10,000 | ₹15,000/mo | Solo founders, small agencies |
| Growth | ₹15,000 | ₹35,000/mo | 10–50 person agencies, SaaS startups |
| Enterprise | Custom | ₹75,000+/mo | High-growth companies, multi-channel |
| Project / One-time | — | ₹25,000–₹75,000 | Custom automation builds |

---

## Why This Pricing Works

### vs. 1 SDR
- 1 junior SDR in India: ₹25,000–₹40,000/month salary
- Add tools (Zoho, Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Nav): ₹8,000–₹15,000/month
- Management time + training: ₹5,000–₹10,000/month equiv.
- **Total SDR cost: ₹38,000–₹65,000/month**
- **JARVIS Growth plan: ₹35,000/month** → cheaper, more consistent, 24/7

### ROI Frame (for sales conversations)
- If our system books 10 calls/month
- Client closes 2 deals at ₹50,000/mo each = ₹1,00,000 new MRR
- Cost: ₹35,000 + ₹15,000 setup (amortized ₹1,250/mo)
- **ROI: 17× in month 1. Ongoing: 2.8× every month.**

## Setup Fee Rationale
- Covers actual build time (3–5 days of setup work)
- Qualifies serious buyers (no freebie seekers)
- Paid upfront = cash flow positive from day 1
- **Never waive setup fee** unless trading for a case study + video testimonial

---

## Free Pilot Strategy
- Offer "7-day free pilot" on Growth plan ONLY for first 3 clients
- Deliverable: system live + first 200 emails sent
- Goal: get results fast → convert to paid → get testimonial
- Condition: prospect must be genuinely qualified (passes ICP filter)

---

## Payment Terms
- Setup fee: 100% upfront before build starts
- Monthly retainer: 1st month upfront, then monthly
- Payment methods: Razorpay / bank transfer / UPI
- No refunds after setup work begins (document in contract)

---

## Discounts Policy
- **No % discounts** — they kill perceived value
- Acceptable: "I'll include the LinkedIn DM module at no extra charge"
- Never discount below cost — walk away from bad-fit clients
