# JARVIS PRIME - Client Acquisition Playbook

## Current Objective

Book the next 5 qualified discovery calls and close the first paid client or paid pilot.

Primary niche: B2B agencies and service companies in India that sell high-ticket services and need a more predictable lead pipeline.

Core offer:

> We build an AI outbound and follow-up system that finds prospects, personalizes outreach, sends follow-ups, and books qualified calls in 7 days.

## Best First Offer

Use this offer for the next 14 days:

> Free outbound audit + 7-day AI pipeline pilot. If we cannot show a clear path to 10 qualified calls per month, you do not pay.

Why this works:

- It sells a business outcome, not "AI".
- It reduces risk for founders who have not bought automation before.
- It creates a natural reason to book a call.
- It lets you collect proof and a testimonial quickly.

## Who To Contact First

Prioritize these prospects:

1. Digital marketing, performance marketing, lead generation, web development, and IT service agencies.
2. Companies with 5-50 employees.
3. Founders, co-founders, CEOs, MDs, heads of growth, or heads of sales.
4. Businesses already selling services worth Rs 25,000+ per client.
5. Businesses showing pain signals: hiring sales roles, posting about growth, running paid ads, offering lead generation, or asking people to book calls.

Avoid for now:

- Very large agencies where decisions move slowly.
- Tiny freelancers with no budget.
- Companies with no clear paid offer.
- Businesses where you cannot find a founder or decision maker.

## Qualification Score

Score every prospect from 0 to 10 before outreach.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Founder or decision maker identified | 2 |
| Agency/service business with B2B clients | 2 |
| Clear contact channel found | 2 |
| Offers lead gen, sales, ads, web, or automation | 2 |
| Has team/social proof/case studies | 1 |
| Shows growth signal or active posting | 1 |

Only spend custom effort on prospects scoring 7+.

## Discovery Call Agenda

Use this structure for every call.

1. What are you selling, and who is the ideal client?
2. How do you currently get new clients?
3. How many qualified calls did you book last month?
4. What happens after a lead shows interest?
5. What tools do you already use? CRM, email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, sheets?
6. What would make this worth paying for in 30 days?
7. Pitch the pilot: "I will build a small version in 7 days and prove the workflow before expanding."

## Pilot Scope

Keep the first pilot small and measurable.

Deliverables:

- 1 ICP document
- 1 lead source workflow
- 100 researched prospects
- 3-message outreach sequence
- AI personalization prompt
- Reply tracking sheet or Supabase view
- Weekly summary report

Success metric:

- 5+ positive replies or 2+ booked calls from the first 100 prospects.

Starter pricing:

- Free audit
- Rs 10,000-15,000 setup for pilot
- Rs 15,000-35,000/month after pilot depending on volume
