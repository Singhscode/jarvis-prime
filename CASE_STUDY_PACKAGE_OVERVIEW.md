**Goal:** Give sales team ammunition to close deals

**Steps:**
1. Download `CASE_STUDY_PDF_READY.md` → convert to PDF

**Time:** 30 minutes setup  
**ROI:** Faster closing + better objection handling

### Strategy 4: Lead Magnet (Week 1+)

**Goal:** Use case study to capture leads

**Steps:**
1. Create gated PDF landing page:
   - Headline: "How Crescendo Ventures Scaled 3x Pipeline in 90 Days"
   - Short description (2–3 sentences)

   - PDF download button
2. Use `CASE_STUDY_PDF_READY.md` as the gated asset
3. Drive traffic via:
   - Email campaigns

   - Social media
   - Partner outreach

**Time:** 1–2 hours to set up gate  
**ROI:** 50–100 qualified leads/month (typical conversion: 10–20% of downloads)

---

### Strategy 5: Email Campaign (Week 2+)

**Goal:** Nurture leads with customer success proof

**Steps:**
1. Use `CASE_STUDY_SOCIAL_BUNDLE.md` → Email Newsletter template
2. Create 3-email sequence:
   - **Email 1:** Hero email (problem + journey)
   - **Email 2:** Results email (metrics + testimonial)
   - **Email 3:** CTA email (free pilot + calendar link)
3. Segment to:
   - Warm leads: Send all 3
   - Cold leads: Send email 1 only
   - Hot leads: Skip to email 3
4. Track open rate, click rate, pilot signups

**Time:** 1 hour to write  
**ROI:** 30–40% open rate, 5–8% click rate, 15–25% of clickers request pilot
