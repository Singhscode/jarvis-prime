RED FLAGS (EARLY WARNING SIGNS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

If you see these, adjust immediately:

🚨 Open rate below 20%
   → Your subject line sucks. Rewrite it.
   → You're not personalizing enough. Research better.

🚨 Reply rate below 1%
   → Your email is too salesy. Dial it back.
   → Your observation is too generic. Get more specific.

🚨 0 demo calls after 100 emails
   → Something is fundamentally wrong.
   → Have someone review your email + subject line.

🚨 0 customers after 50 demo calls
   → Your offer is confusing or price is wrong.
   → Recording a demo call and reviewing it.

🚨 Customer doesn't hit 8 meetings in 60 days
   → You didn't deliver on guarantee.
   → Work for free or offer refund.
   → This is a learning experience, not a loss.
