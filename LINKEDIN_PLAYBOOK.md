## PART 4: DAILY ENGAGEMENT ROUTINE (30 min)

### Step 1: Engage on Your Posts (10 min)

Within 24 hours of posting:
- [ ] Like every comment
- [ ] Reply to every comment with value (not just "thanks!")
- [ ] Ask follow-up questions to encourage more comments

**Why:** Algorithm rewards engagement. More comments = more reach.

---

### Step 2: Engage on Prospects' Posts (10 min)

Find 5-10 prospects and:
- [ ] Like 1 recent post each
- [ ] Leave a thoughtful comment (2-3 sentences, adds value)
- [ ] Don't mention your product

**Comment template:**
```
Great take on [topic].

One thing we're seeing with [type of company]: [relevant insight].

Curious what you're seeing on your end.

—Anuj
```

**Why:** They'll notice you. Some will visit your profile. Some will be curious and reach out.

**Message:**

## PART 8: COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Mistake 1:** Posting too often (more than 3x/week = looks spammy)  
✅ **Fix:** Stick to 2-3x/week. Quality > quantity.

❌ **Mistake 2:** Only promoting your product  
✅ **Fix:** 80% value, 20% pitch. Give first, ask second.

❌ **Mistake 3:** Not engaging with others  
✅ **Fix:** 30 min daily engagement (your posts won't grow without it).

❌ **Mistake 4:** Posting generic advice  
✅ **Fix:** Share specific data, specific stories, specific lessons.

❌ **Mistake 5:** Not following up with commenters  
✅ **Fix:** Reply to every comment within 24 hours.
