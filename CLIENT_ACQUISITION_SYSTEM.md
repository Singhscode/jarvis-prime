# LEAD SOURCING WORKFLOW

## 5A. Automated Lead Sourcing Process

### Overview

**Goal:** Find 500 qualified prospects per month, automatically

**Channels:**
- LinkedIn (15% of leads)
- ZoomInfo (35% of leads)
- Hunter.io (25% of leads)
- Apollo.io (15% of leads)
- Manual research (10% of leads)

---

### Complete Workflow (Day-by-Day)

**Day 1: Define Audience**

```
Input your ICP:
├─ Company size: 5-50 employees
├─ Revenue: ₹5L–50L
├─ Industry: SaaS, Agencies, B2B
├─ Geography: India + US
└─ Hiring signals: "VP Sales", "SDR hired", "Scaling"
```

---

**Day 2: Bulk Source via ZoomInfo**

```
1. Log into ZoomInfo
2. Create saved search:
   - Company size: 5-50
   - Industry: [Your ICP industries]
   - Revenue: [Your ICP revenue]
   - Location: [Your ICP locations]
   - Recent hiring: Sales/SDR/VP Sales
3. Export: CSV with 200+ leads
```

---

**Day 3: Secondary Source via Hunter.io**

```
1. Log into Hunter.io
2. Search companies by keyword:
   - "B2B SaaS" + location
   - "Sales agency" + location
   - "Digital agency" + location
3. For each company, find all emails:
   - [firstname@company.com](mailto:firstname@company.com)
   - [Sales team emails]
   - [Founder emails]
4. Export: CSV with 150+ leads
```

---

**Day 4: LinkedIn Sourcing**

```
1. Log into LinkedIn (with Sales Navigator subscription)
2. Create saved search:
   - Title: "Founder" OR "CEO" OR "VP Sales" OR "Head of Sales"
   - Company size: 5-50
   - Industry: [Your industries]
   - Location: [Your locations]
3. Export: CSV with 100+ leads
```

---

**Day 5: Data Enrichment**

```
Run all leads through enrichment tools:

1. Email verification (Neverbounce / RocketReach)
   - Filter: Valid emails only
2. Phone lookup (RocketReach)
   - Get phone numbers where available
3. LinkedIn enrichment (ZoomInfo)
   - Get LinkedIn URLs
   - Get recent job changes
4. Company research (Crunchbase)
   - Get funding info
   - Get recent news

Output: CSV with enriched data
```

---

**Day 6: ICP Scoring & Filtering**

```
Run all leads through ICP scoring:

Use your ICP scorer (built in earlier):
├─ Revenue score (0-10)
├─ Keyword match score (0-8)
├─ Title score (0-3)
├─ Engagement signals (if available)
└─ Total score: 0-25

Keep only:
├─ Score 18+: Hot prospects (send immediately)
├─ Score 15-17: Qualified prospects (add to sequence)
└─ Score <15: Remove from list

Expected result: 300-400 qualified leads (from 500+ sourced)
```

---

**Day 7: Setup Sequences & Send**

```
1. Import qualified leads into email platform:
   - Lemlist, Instantly, Mailchimp, or custom n8n
2. Select sequence based on ICP score:
   - Score 20+: "Growth-Focused" sequence
   - Score 15-19: "Revenue-Focused" sequence
3. Set send schedule:
   - Batch 1: 100 emails at 9 AM
   - Batch 2: 100 emails at 1 PM
   - Batch 3: 100 emails at 5 PM
4. Enable tracking:
   - Track opens, clicks, replies
   - Auto-route engaged leads to Slack
```

→ Action: Review their ICP, sales process; offer sales support
