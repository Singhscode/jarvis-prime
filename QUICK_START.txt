OBSTACLES & SOLUTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROBLEM: "I'm not getting responses to cold emails"
SOLUTION: 
  • Personalize more (mention something specific)
  • Change subject line (test different angles)
  • Better targeting (make sure they're agency owners)
  • Follow up 3 times (most sales in follow-ups)

PROBLEM: "They say yes on the call but don't sign"
SOLUTION:
  • Lower the price ($1,000 if needed for first 3)
  • Remove the contract (handshake deal)
  • Offer 7-day free trial
  • Better objection handling

PROBLEM: "I can't deliver the results"
SOLUTION:
  • Make sure ICP is clear (ask more questions)
  • Test different email angles
  • Send more emails (volume matters)
  • Follow up better (call after 5 days)

PROBLEM: "I'm getting exhausted"
SOLUTION:
  • Batch the work (all cold emails same time)
  • Use templates (don't write everything from scratch)
  • Automate tracking (spreadsheet formula)
  • Take breaks (rest is productive)

PROBLEM: "My first customer doesn't hit 8 meetings"
SOLUTION:
  • Honor the guarantee (give month free)
  • Keep trying (don't give up)
  • Learn what didn't work
  • Apply learnings to customer #2
  • You'll get better each time

1. FOCUS ONLY ON MARKETING AGENCIES

5. SHIP BEFORE PERFECT
   Don't wait. Launch now. Iterate based on feedback.

   That's it.

7. TRACK EVERYTHING
   If you can't measure it, you can't improve it.
   Spreadsheet is your best friend.

8. FOLLOW UP RELENTLESSLY
   80% of sales come from follow-ups.
   Most people quit after 1 try.
   You don't.
