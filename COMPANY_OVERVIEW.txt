WHAT IS JARVIS PRIME?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AI-powered outbound sales automation platform that helps agencies and B2B 
companies book qualified sales meetings without hiring SDRs.

Simple version: "We send cold emails and book meetings for you automatically."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CORE SERVICES (3 MAIN OFFERINGS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SERVICE 1: AI APPOINTMENT SETTING
What it does:
  • Finds qualified prospects matching your ideal customer profile
  • Sends personalized cold email sequences
  • Sends personalized LinkedIn messages
  • Tracks opens, clicks, replies
  • Books meetings directly on your calendar
  • Alerts founder via Telegram when meeting booked

Cost: $1,200-2,000/month
Results: 10-20 qualified meetings per month


SERVICE 2: AI VOICE AGENTS
What it does:
  • Handles inbound phone calls (qualify leads)
  • Makes outbound calls (appointment setting)
  • Transcribes conversations
  • Updates CRM automatically
  • 24/7 availability

Cost: Included in Professional & Enterprise tiers
Results: Automated call handling, higher contact rate


SERVICE 3: WORKFLOW AUTOMATION
What it does:
  • CRM integration (HubSpot, Salesforce)
  • Automatic data syncing
  • Lead scoring (0-30 scale)
  • Lead routing to sales team
  • Performance dashboards
  • Reporting automation

Cost: Included in Professional & Enterprise tiers
Results: Eliminates manual data entry, faster sales cycle

      - Qualifies them
      - Scores against ICP
      - Routes to CRM

      - Scores leads 0-30 points
      - Company size scoring
      - Tech stack analysis
      - LinkedIn verification
      - Apollo.io enrichment
    
    • apollo.js (NEW - Recently Added)
      - Apollo.io API client
      - Search people/companies
      - Enrichment by email
      - Export contacts
      - Technology stack detection


INTEGRATIONS (/agents/src/lib)
  • supabase.js — Database for storing customers, leads, prospects
  • ai.js — Groq AI for generating personalized messages
  • resend.js — Sending emails at scale
  • telegram.js — Sending alerts to founder
  • email-verifier.js — Validates email addresses
