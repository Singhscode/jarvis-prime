The Problem: The SDR Treadmill

By Q4 2025, Crescendo Ventures was stuck. They had 8 SDRs on payroll (₹35L/year), but were only generating 3–5 meetings per month. The pipeline was inconsistent, the process was manual, and scaling seemed impossible without hiring more people they couldn't afford.

The Real Issue:

  • Inconsistent pipeline: 15–30 leads/month with no predictability
  • Manual processes: Spreadsheets, copy-paste sequences, no personalization at scale
  • No scoring system: All leads treated equally (good and bad)
  • High turnover: SDRs burning out, 40% annual turnover
  • Growth plateau: Stuck at 8% YoY growth (down from 20% two years prior)

They faced a tough choice: Hire more SDRs (expensive, unproven ROI) or automate and optimize (risky, untested).

They chose automation.

The Solution: JARVIS PRIME Automation

Week 1–2: Discovery

JARVIS worked with Crescendo to define their ideal customer profile (ICP), audit their existing sequences, and identify the best prospect sources. Result: 2,500 qualified prospects identified, 280 high-fit targets prioritized.

Week 3–5: Setup

JARVIS built:

  • Custom ICP scoring engine (0–25 scale)
  • 5 different email sequences for different buyer personas
  • AI-powered personalization engine (research, news, timing)
  • Automated lead routing and scoring

Week 6–12: Execution & Optimization

  • Week 1–4: Sent 280 personalized emails → 8 meetings booked
  • Week 5–8: Sent 300 more emails + LinkedIn outreach → 18 meetings (cumulative)
  • Week 9–12: Sent 350 more emails + re-engagement sequences → 35+ meetings (cumulative)

The key: Same message, same personalization, same timing for every prospect. Consistency beats heroics.
