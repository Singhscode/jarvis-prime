## SECTION 2: EMAIL OUTREACH

### Q: Why is my open rate low (<25%)?

**A:** Three possible causes:

**Cause 1: Deliverability issue**
- Emails going to spam folder
- Fix: Use email testing tool (mail-tester.com)
- Solution: Warm up domain, check SPF/DKIM records

**Cause 2: Subject line weak**
- People not opening because subject doesn't compel
- Fix: A/B test 3 different subjects on next 100 emails
- Solution: Use specific data in subject ("Noticed you hired VP Sales")

**Cause 3: Audience wrong**
- Opening to list of bad emails (typos, fake addresses)
- Fix: Re-score prospects, remove low ICP scores
- Solution: Use better data source (ZoomInfo > Hunter > Apollo)

**Action:** Fix one of these. Re-test for 100 emails. Check results.

---

### Q: Why is my reply rate low (<2%)?

**A:** Four possible causes:

**Cause 1: Email copy is weak**
- Value prop not clear
- Fix: Review best-performing sequence from case studies
- Solution: Simplify opening, make benefit obvious

**Cause 2: Personalization not working**
- Email feels generic despite [PLACEHOLDERS]
- Fix: Check that [COMPANY], [FIRST_NAME], etc are actually being replaced
- Solution: Add more specific research (recent hire, recent funding)

**Cause 3: Audience fit is wrong**
- Right company, wrong person
- Fix: Re-score for decision-maker titles
- Solution: Use better targeting (CEO/Founder > Manager)

**Cause 4: CTA is weak**
- Not clear what you want them to do
- Fix: Make it explicit ("Reply Yes/No" vs "Let me know")
- Solution: Add calendar link directly in email

**Action:** Fix #1 or #2 first (easiest). Re-test for 100 emails.

---

### Q: Should I send on weekdays or weekends?

**A:** 

**Best:** Tuesday-Thursday, 8-9 AM IST  
**Good:** Monday 9 AM (second best)  
**Okay:** Friday 9 AM (still works)  
**Avoid:** Friday afternoon (gets buried)  
**Don't:** Weekends (professional emails on work time)

**Test:** Send 50 emails on different days. Track which perform best for your audience.

---

### Q: How many emails can I send per day without looking spammy?

**A:**

**Safe:** 100-200/day (target: 100)  
**Aggressive:** 300-500/day  
**Risky:** 1,000+/day  

**Key:** Depends on YOUR domain.

**If new domain:**
- Week 1-2: Send 50/day (warm up domain)
- Week 3+: Send 100+/day (safe)

**If established domain:**
- Send 100+/day immediately

**Monitor:** Check spam reports. If >2% unsubscribe, slow down.

## SECTION 3: LINKEDIN STRATEGY

### Q: Should I post daily or 3x/week?

**A:** 

**Start:** 3x/week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 8 AM)  
**Reason:** Quality > quantity. One great post > three mediocre posts.

**If scaling:** Can increase to 5x/week once you've figured out what works.

**Don't:** Post more than once per day (looks spammy).

---

### Q: What if I don't get comments on my posts?

**A:**

**Week 1-2:** Normal. Building audience.  
**Week 3+:** Should see 5-20 comments per post.

**If not getting comments:**
1) Change post time (try 12 PM instead of 8 AM)
2) Change post content (case studies get most engagement)
3) Increase engagement on others' posts (comment first, get comments back)
4) Add clear CTA (end with question)

**Pro tip:** Comment on other people's posts 15 min after posting yours. Gets early engagement signals to algorithm.

---

### Q: How do I grow followers faster?

**A:**

**What works:**
- Post consistently (3x/week)
- Engage on others' posts (15 min/day)
- Connect with 10 prospects daily (warm connections)
- Participate in relevant LinkedIn groups

**What doesn't work:**
- Follow/unfollow loops (spammy)
- Buying followers (fake)
- Posting multiple times per day (annoying)
- Only promoting your product (boring)

**Expected timeline:**
- Month 1: 100-200 followers
- Month 2: 300-500 followers
- Month 3: 500-1,000 followers
- Month 6: 1,000-2,000 followers

### Q: Should I message people who view my profile?

**Template:**

```
Noticed [specific fact about them]. 

Think we should know each other. Just connected.

```

## SECTION 4: SALES CALLS

### Q: How long should discovery calls be?

**A:** 

**Target:** 20 minutes (15-25 min is fine)  
**Not:** 30+ minutes (too long, schedule separately)  

**If they want longer:** "How about we do a 20-min call today, and if we need more time, we can schedule a follow-up?"

---

### Q: What if they don't answer the call?

**A:**

**If on Calendly:** They get reminder 24 hours before, then 15 min before. Some won't show.

**Your action:**
1) Send follow-up email same day ("I had us on calendar, let's reschedule")
2) Wait 2 days
3) If no response, assume not interested

**Attendance rate:**
- Expect 75-80% to show up
- 20-25% will no-show or reschedule

---

### Q: How many calls before I get a deal?

**A:** 

**Typical conversion funnel:**
```
Email → Reply (4%) → Call (50% of replies) → Proposal (60% of calls) → Deal (30% of proposals)

So: 100 emails → 4 replies → 2 calls → 1.2 proposals → 0.36 deals

In other words: 1 deal per ~280 emails
Or: 1 deal per 5-7 sales calls
```

**Real numbers from Crescendo case study:**
- 500 emails → 20 replies → 15 calls → 5 proposals → 1-2 deals closed (25-40% close rate)

---

### Q: When should I send the proposal?

**A:** 

**Best:** Same day after call (while momentum is high)  
**Good:** Next morning (still fresh)  
**Weak:** More than 24 hours later (momentum dies)

**Template:** Use the Proposal Template from SALES_PLAYBOOK.md (customize with their info)

### Q: What if they say "Let me think about it"?

**A:**

**Don't:** Let them just disappear  
**Do:** Send resources + schedule follow-up call

**Script:**

**Fast:** 2-3 weeks (rare, usually enthusiastic founder)

## SECTION 5: METRICS & TROUBLESHOOTING

### Q: What if I'm hitting all my metrics but not closing deals?

**A:** 

**You have a sales problem, not a lead problem.**

Check:
1) Are you actually having discovery calls? (or just booking them?)
2) Are your proposals professional? (or rushed?)
3) Are you following up persistently? (or one and done?)
4) Is your offer clear? (or are people confused?)

**Fix:**
- Record a call, listen back (assess quality)
- Review proposal quality (get feedback from buyer)
- Add daily follow-ups (vs weekly)
- Clarify pricing + investment + timeline

---

### Q: What if I'm getting great metrics but wrong type of leads?

**A:**

**Problem:** Wrong ICP (getting bad fit companies)

**Diagnosis:**
- Are these companies actually in your target size/revenue?
- Are these actually decision makers?
- Are they in your target industry?

**Fix:**
1) Re-run ICP workshop (maybe your ICP is wrong)
2) Re-score all prospects
3) Remove bad fits from list
4) Restart with better list

---

### Q: How do I know if I should scale or optimize?

**A:**

**You're ready to SCALE if:**
- Open rate: 35%+
- Reply rate: 3%+
- Close rate: 25%+
- Customer happy: 8+/10
- You have sales bandwidth

**Action:** 2x emails/week, same system, expect 2x results.

- Open rate: <30%

## SECTION 7: COMMON MISTAKES

### Mistake 1: Giving up too soon

**Problem:** Week 1-2, "This isn't working."  
**Fix:** Give it 90 days. Results come late in Month 1 / early Month 2.

---

### Mistake 2: Changing sequences constantly

**Problem:** Using Sequence A for 20 emails, then switching to B.  
**Fix:** Commit to 100+ emails per sequence before judging.

---

### Mistake 3: Wrong list

**Problem:** Sending to bad emails, fake companies, wrong industries.  
**Fix:** Spend Week 1 building GREAT list. Quality > quantity.

---

### Mistake 4: Not following up

**Problem:** Sending emails but not replying to replies.  
**Fix:** Reply within 2 hours to every reply. Every. Single. One.

---

### Mistake 5: No social proof

**Problem:** Cold email with no context, no proof, no reason to believe.  
**Fix:** Add case study, add social proof, add specific social signal.

---

### Mistake 6: Weak CTA

**Problem:** "Let me know if interested" vs "Book here: [link]"  
**Fix:** Make it stupid easy. Calendar link in every email.

---

### Mistake 7: Giving up on LinkedIn

**Problem:** Posted once, got 0 engagement, never posted again.  
**Fix:** Post 3x/week for 8 weeks before judging.
