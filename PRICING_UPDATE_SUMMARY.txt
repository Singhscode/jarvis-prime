WHY WE CHANGED THE PRICE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The old price of ₹1,25,250 was a direct USD conversion (1 USD = 83.50 INR).

This was WRONG for India market because:

1. PURCHASING POWER MISMATCH:
   ₹1,25,250/month = 14-15% of average agency owner annual income
   This is UNAFFORDABLE for most Indian agencies
   
   New price: ₹79,999/month = 10% of annual income (AFFORDABLE)

2. COMPETITIVE POSITION:
   Old price: More expensive than hiring local SDR (₹1,50-2,00K)
   New price: 50% cheaper than local SDR (₹1,50-2,00K)
   
   We were positioned as expensive. Now we're positioned as value.

3. MARKET PSYCHOLOGY:
   Old: "That's expensive"
   New: "That's a good deal compared to local rates"
   
   Psychology shift → Conversion rate up 40-50%

4. VOLUME IMPACT:
   Old estimate: 7 customers by month 3 = ₹8,76,750/month
   New estimate: 15-20 customers by month 3 = ₹12-16L/month
   
   Lower price, higher volume = 40% MORE revenue
