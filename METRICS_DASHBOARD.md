## DASHBOARD ALERTS & RED FLAGS

### 🚨 RED FLAG: Low Open Rate (<25%)

**What it means:** Emails not resonating or going to spam  
**Check first:** Deliverability (email testing tool)  
**Fix:** Change subject lines, change send time, improve ICP  
**Timeline to fix:** 3-5 days (test 100 emails)  
**Action:** Implement changes, re-test

---

### 🚨 RED FLAG: Low Reply Rate (<2%)

**What it means:** Email copy weak or audience wrong  
**Check first:** Sample of received emails (did they get them?)  
**Fix:** Test different email copy, improve personalization, better ICP  
**Timeline to fix:** 5-7 days  
**Action:** A/B test sequences

---

### 🚨 RED FLAG: Meetings Scheduled But Not Showing Up (Attendance <60%)

**What it means:** People booking but not showing up  
**Check first:** Reminder email working? Calendar working?  
**Fix:** Send reminder 24 hours before, send day-of reminder, phone call 1 hour before  
**Timeline to fix:** Immediate (test next call)  
**Action:** Add 2 reminder touchpoints

---

### 🚨 RED FLAG: Meetings Held But No Deals (Close Rate <15%)

**What it means:** Meetings happening but not converting  
**Check first:** Sales team doing discovery properly? Proposal quality?  
**Fix:** Better discovery call script, better proposal, faster follow-up  
**Timeline to fix:** 5-7 days  
**Action:** Review recordings, improve script

---

### 🚨 RED FLAG: Low LinkedIn Engagement (<2%)

**What it means:** Posts not resonating with audience  
**Check first:** Is audience right? Is timing right? Is content valuable?  
**Fix:** Change content type (test case studies), change time, improve quality  
**Timeline to fix:** 5-10 days (test 3 posts)  
**Action:** Focus on best-performing content types

---

### 🟡 YELLOW FLAG: Slower Than Target (But Still Moving)

**Emails:** 60/day instead of 100/day → Increase to 100 by end of week  
**Meetings:** 5 instead of 8 → Review leads, improve follow-up  
**Followers:** 10/week instead of 30/week → Post more, engage more

## METRICS YOU SHOULD AUTOMATE

### Auto-Calculate These (Put in Google Sheet Formulas)

```
Open Rate = Opens ÷ Emails Sent × 100
Click Rate = Clicks ÷ Emails Sent × 100
Reply Rate = Replies ÷ Emails Sent × 100
Meeting Rate = Meetings ÷ Replies × 100
Close Rate = Deals Closed ÷ Proposals Sent × 100
Cost Per Lead = Investment ÷ Qualified Leads
Cost Per Meeting = Investment ÷ Meetings Booked
Cost Per Deal = Investment ÷ Deals Closed
ROI = (Return - Investment) ÷ Investment × 100
Payback Period = Investment ÷ Monthly Return (in months)
```
