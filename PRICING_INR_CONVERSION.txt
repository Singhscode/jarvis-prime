PRICING PSYCHOLOGY (Why ₹1,25,250 works)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY NOT ROUND NUMBERS?

₹1,25,000 feels too round (like placeholder pricing)
₹1,25,250 feels premium and calculated
  ✓ Shows you've thought about value
  ✓ Feels more legitimate
  ✓ Harder to negotiate down
  ✓ Signals confidence

WHY NOT HIGHER/LOWER?

Too low (₹50,000-75,000):
  ❌ Signals low value
  ❌ Customers think "cheap = bad"
  ❌ They won't take you seriously
  ❌ Low profit = can't hire

Too high (₹2,00,000-3,00,000):
  ❌ No customers sign up
  ❌ Better to start proven, then raise price
  ❌ You can't deliver at that scale solo

Just right (₹1,25,250):
  ✓ Premium (not cheap)
  ✓ Accessible (agencies can afford it)
  ✓ Profitable (you make money)
  ✓ Defensible (worth it for guarantee)
