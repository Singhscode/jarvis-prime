PURCHASING POWER PARITY ANALYSIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Problem with Simple USD Conversion:

USD Price: $1,500/month
Converted: ₹1,25,250/month (using direct 1 USD = 83.50 INR)

But this ignores purchasing power differences:

In USA:
  • Agency owner salary: $80K-200K/year
  • Your service cost: 9-18% of salary
  • Acceptable range: 5-15%

In India:
  • Agency owner salary: $12K-36K/year (equivalent purchasing power role)
  • Converted price cost: 50-150% of salary (UNAFFORDABLE)
  • Acceptable range: 5-15% = ₹50K-90K/month

PURCHASING POWER PARITY CALCULATION:

Proper conversion factor for SaaS/services: 1:50 to 1:40 (not 1:83.5)

$1,500 × 50 = ₹75,000 (more realistic for Indian market)
$1,500 × 40 = ₹60,000 (aggressive pricing)
