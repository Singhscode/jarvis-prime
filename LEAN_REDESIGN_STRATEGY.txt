═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TRAP #1: "Let me hire a sales team first"
  ❌ Wrong: You won't know how to manage them
  ✓ Right: Sell yourself first. Learn the process. Then hire.

TRAP #2: "Let me build more features first"
  ❌ Wrong: Features don't sell. Results do.
  ✓ Right: Sell what you have. Build based on feedback.

TRAP #3: "Let me get testimonials before launch"
  ❌ Wrong: You won't have customers yet
  ✓ Right: Launch now. Testimonials come later.

TRAP #4: "Let me improve my website more"
  ❌ Wrong: Website doesn't matter. Customers do.
  ✓ Right: One-page site is fine. Go sell.

TRAP #5: "Let me wait until I have a case study"
  ❌ Wrong: You need to START somewhere
  ✓ Right: Your first customer IS your case study.

TRAP #6: "Let me perfect the guarantee"
  ❌ Wrong: Guarantees are simple. Stop overthinking.
  ✓ Right: "8+ meetings or month free." Done.

TRAP #7: "Let me build the AI backend first"
  ❌ Wrong: You don't need AI. You need customers.
  ✓ Right: Run campaigns manually. Automate later.
