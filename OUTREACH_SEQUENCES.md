## 🎬 Pro Tips

### Email Write Tips:
1. **Keep it short** — 100-150 words max
2. **Start with "why"** — Why are you reaching out NOW?
3. **Use their language** — If they said "scale", use "scale" not "growth"
4. **Be specific** — "Recently hired 5 people" not "You're growing"
5. **Ask a question** — End with curiosity, not a pitch
6. **Include a pattern interrupt** — Something that makes them think
7. **Make the CTA easy** — Calendar link, not "let me know"

### Personalization Hacks:
1. **Find 1 specific fact** — Recent post, funding, product launch, hire
2. **Mention it in line 1** — Proves you researched
3. **Connect it to your offer** — Why this fact = why now
4. **Make it specific** — Not "saw you're scaling" but "saw you just hired VP of Sales"

### Timing Tips:
1. **Send early morning** — 8-9 AM IST
2. **Tuesday-Thursday best** — Monday too busy, Friday too late
3. **Avoid Monday-Friday 12-1 PM** — Lunch time
4. **Never send Friday evening** — Gets buried in weekend inbox
