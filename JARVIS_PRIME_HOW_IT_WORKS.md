## 💡 Key Success Factors

✅ **Clear ICP**: Tight definition = higher quality leads  
✅ **Authentic Outreach**: Personalization matters  
✅ **Fast Responses**: Reply within 1 hour for best results  
✅ **Strong Value Prop**: Why should they care?  
✅ **Easy Booking**: Make scheduling frictionless  
✅ **Consistent Follow-up**: Don't give up after 1st email  
✅ **CRM Integration**: Track everything  
✅ **Regular Optimization**: A/B test subject lines, CTAs  

---

## ❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Vague ICP**: "Anyone in B2B" ← Too broad  
❌ **Generic Emails**: "Hey, I think we could work together"  
❌ **Too Many Sequences**: Overwhelming prospects  
❌ **Poor Follow-up**: Giving up too early  
❌ **Slow Response Time**: "I'll get back to you next week"  
❌ **Bad Calendar Link**: Hard to book  
❌ **No CRM Tracking**: Lost opportunities  
❌ **Not Optimizing**: Assuming same message works for all
