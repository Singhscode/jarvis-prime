TEMPLATE #2: DEMO CALL SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DURATION: 30 minutes exactly
STRUCTURE: 4 parts (5-7-10-8 minutes)

---

PART 1: RAPPORT & CONTEXT (5 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

YOU: "Hey [First Name]! Thanks for jumping on. Before we dive in, I'm curious — 
      what made you want to take this call?"

[Listen. They'll mention a pain point. Write it down.]

YOU: "Got it. And just out of curiosity, how are you currently getting leads?"

[Listen. They'll describe their process.]

YOU: "Cool. So you're [summarize what they said]. Makes sense."


PART 2: THEIR PROBLEM (7 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

YOU: "Here's what I'm hearing: you need consistent pipeline without 
      [problem they mentioned]. Is that fair?"

[They say yes]

YOU: "Most agencies I talk to are in the same spot. They spend $4-8K/month on 
      an SDR who takes 2-3 months to ramp, or they use tools that are a pain 
      to manage.

      What's your current situation like?"

[Listen. Let them talk 80/20.]

YOU: "If I could show you a way to get 8-15 meetings per month with zero hiring 
      headaches, would that be interesting?"

[They say yes]


PART 3: THE OFFER (10 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

YOU: "Here's how we work:

      Month 1: We do research, write emails, manage outreach
      Result: 8-15 qualified meetings for you

      You only do one thing: show up to the calls.

      Cost: ₹1,25,250/month
      Guarantee: 8+ meetings in 60 days or we work for free

      What questions do you have?"

[Pause. Listen to their objection.]

COMMON OBJECTIONS & RESPONSES:

OBJECTION: "How is this different from using Apollo + Lemlist?"
YOU: "Good question. Apollo finds leads. Lemlist sends emails. But somebody 
      has to: research your ICP, write the emails, manage follow-ups, qualify 
      responses, book your meetings. 
      
      That 'somebody' is usually you, taking 10 hours/week. Or you hire an SDR 
      for $4K-8K/month who takes 3 months to ramp.
      
      We handle all of it. You just show up to meetings. That's the difference."

OBJECTION: "What if it doesn't work?"
YOU: "Then we work for free until it does. We guarantee 8+ meetings in 60 days. 
      If we don't hit that, you don't pay. We only win when you win. Simple."

OBJECTION: "Can I cancel anytime?"
YOU: "Yep. Month-to-month. If we're not delivering, cancel. But we're confident 
      in our process."

OBJECTION: "I need to think about it"
YOU: "Totally fair. Here's what I'd suggest: Let's do a quick audit of your 
      ideal prospects. Takes 15 minutes. Then you can decide if this makes 
      sense. Sound good?"

[If they say yes, do a quick ICP audit in next 5 minutes]

[If they say no, get email and follow up in 3 days]


PART 4: CLOSING (8 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IF THEY'RE INTERESTED:

YOU: "Perfect. Here's next steps:

      1. I'll send you a one-page agreement
      2. You sign and send back
      3. We do a 30-min kickoff call Monday
      4. We start research Tuesday
      5. First emails go out Thursday
      6. First meetings come in by mid-next week

      Does that timeline work?"

[If yes, send agreement]

YOU: "Great. Anything else before I send the agreement over?"

[If they ask questions, answer them]

YOU: "You're going to be happy with this. I promise. Send me the signed 
      agreement and we'll get rolling."

IF THEY'RE NOT INTERESTED:

YOU: "No problem. I get it. Here's what I'd suggest: 

      If lead generation becomes an issue in the next 30 days, reach out. 
      I'll run a free audit of your ideal prospects. Deal?"

[They say yes]

YOU: "Great. I'll send you my calendar link. Grab my coffee link if you need it.

      Thanks for the time today."

[Send calendar link]


CALL TRACKING:

After every call, note:

□ Company name
□ Founder name
□ Problem they mentioned
□ Objection (if any)
□ Whether they're interested
□ Follow-up date

This helps you refine your pitch.

WHY THIS FORMAT:

✓ Short (takes 30 seconds to read)
✓ Specific numbers (not vague)

✓ Builds confidence (shows activity)


WEEKLY REPORT CHECKLIST:

Before sending, you should have:

□ Actual email count (not estimated)
□ Real open rate data (from email tool)
□ Real reply rate (calculated from replies)
□ Actual meetings booked (check calendar)
□ Next week's plan (specific, not vague)
□ One positive thing to highlight

OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Keep this handy during demo calls. These are the 80/20 objections.

---

OBJECTION #1: "How do I know this will work for my agency?"

YOU: "Valid question. Here's what I'd say: The process works because:

      1. We research your actual ideal clients (not guessing)
      2. We write personal emails (not templates)
      3. We handle objections in follow-ups
      4. We qualify before you talk to anyone
      
      The only variable is your offer. If your service solves a real problem 
      for real people at prices they can afford, meetings happen.
      
      We've done this 10+ times. Same process. Works every time.

      What's your concern specifically?"

---

OBJECTION #2: "Isn't this just spam?"

YOU: "Not at all. Here's the difference:

      Spam: Mass emails to random people
      Ours: Personalized emails to specific people in your ideal market
      
      Example: Instead of 'Hey, want marketing help?' we send:
      'Hey [Name], I saw you work with [specific type of client]. 
       Most [your niche] agencies struggle with [specific pain]. 
       We help with that.'
      
      It's research-based, not blast-based.

      The open rate proves it: 30-40% (vs 2-5% for spam).

      Make sense?"

---

OBJECTION #3: "Can you guarantee this will work for my specific niche?"

YOU: "Here's what I guarantee: Process and effort.

      Process: The same research-based, personalized approach we use for 
      every client.

      Effort: We send 50-100 emails/week and manage follow-ups.

      The guarantee is: 8+ meetings in 60 days or we work for free.

      That said, if your offer doesn't have a real market (like selling 
      ice to Eskimos), meetings might be hard. But that's a business problem, 
      not an execution problem.

      In our kickoff call, we'll validate your market. If it's weak, we'll 
      tell you straight up.

      Sound fair?"

---

OBJECTION #4: "Isn't ₹1,25,250/month expensive?"

YOU: "Let's math it out:

      Option A: Hire an SDR
      - Cost: ₹1.5-3L/month in salary
      - Ramp time: 2-3 months
      - Success rate: 50% actually work out
      - Effort: You manage, hire, train, fire
      
      Option B: Buy Apollo + Lemlist
      - Cost: ₹5-8K/month in tools
      - Your time: 10-15 hours/week managing campaigns
      - Success rate: Depends on your copywriting skills
      - Effort: You do the work
      
      Option C: JARVIS PRIME
      - Cost: ₹1,25,250/month
      - Your time: 0 hours/week
      - Success rate: Guaranteed 8+ meetings
      - Effort: You show up to meetings
      
      Worst case: You get 8 meetings. If 1 closes at average deal size, 
      ROI is 3-10x.

      Does that make sense?"

---

OBJECTION #5: "I want to think about it"

YOU: "I get it. One thing before you go though:

      Want me to do a quick 15-min audit of your ideal prospects? 
      Takes no time, gives you solid data to think about.

      I'll show you exactly who we'd target and what we'd say to them.

      Then you can decide with real information.

      Interested?"

[If yes: do mini-audit on the call]

[If no: Get their email and follow up in 3 days]

---

HOW TO USE THIS SCRIPT:

1. Listen to their objection completely
2. Acknowledge it ("Valid question" or "I get it")
3. Reframe the issue
4. Provide proof/logic
5. Ask clarifying question
6. Move forward
