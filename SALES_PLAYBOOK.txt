DEMO CALL SCRIPT (20 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[When they join Zoom]

"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time. How's your day going?"
[Listen - 30 seconds]

"Quick agenda for today:
1. I want to understand your agency and your sales challenge (5 min)
2. I'll show you how we help agencies like yours (7 min)
3. We'll see if it's a fit (5 min)
4. If it is, we'll talk next steps (3 min)

Sound good?"

[Listen for yes]

DISCOVERY (5 minutes):

"Tell me about [Agency Name]. What do you guys do?"
[Listen fully - they talk 1-2 min]

"And what does your ideal client look like? Like, describe them."
[Listen - they describe ICP - 1 min]

"How many of those people would you want to meet with per month to hit your 
growth goals?"
[Listen - typically 10-20]

"And what's been your biggest challenge with getting in front of those people?"
[Listen - record their answer]

"Why does that matter to you? Like, what changes if you hit [X meetings/month]?"
[Listen - get emotional reason]

PITCH (7 minutes):

"Okay, so the challenge is [their challenge]. Here's what we do:

We find people who match your ideal client profile. Then we send them 
personalized cold emails. Then we book the meetings directly on your calendar.

That's it. 

Most agencies we work with see 8-15 of those meetings in their first month.

We guarantee it. If we don't deliver 8 qualified meetings in 60 days, your 
first month is free.

You literally just show up and close deals. We handle the rest."

QUALIFICATION (5 minutes):

"Does that sound like something worth exploring?"
[If YES, continue. If NO, ask why]

If they say YES:

"Great. Here's how it works:
1. We start Monday (or [day])
2. First week, we understand your ICP better
3. Week 2-4, we run the campaign
4. We send you a report every Friday
5. In 60 days, you either have 8+ meetings or your money back

Cost is $1,500/month. Month-to-month, so you can cancel anytime.

How does that sound?"

CLOSE (3 minutes):

If they're ready: "Perfect. Let me send you a contract. We'll start Monday."

If they need time: "No problem. Take 24 hours. I'll follow up tomorrow. But be 
honest - is this something you want to do or are you passing?"

If they're hesitant: "What's making you hesitate? Let's talk through it."

FIRST CUSTOMER KICKOFF CALL (30 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AGENDA:

1. Welcome (2 min)
   "Thanks for signing up. Today we're going to nail down exactly who we're 
    targeting so we can start finding them."

2. Deep dive on ICP (15 min)
   Questions:
   • "Walk me through your ideal client. Company size?"
   • "What's their pain point that you solve?"
   • "What's their title/role (who makes the decision)?"
   • "How much do they spend with you typically?"
   • "Why do they choose you over competitors?"
   • "Do you have past clients that are perfect? Tell me about them."
   
   Write everything down. Be specific.

3. Campaign brief (10 min)
   "Okay, so here's what I'm hearing:
   • Company size: X-Y people
   • Industry: [industry]
   • Title: [role]
   • Pain: [problem]
   
   Is that right?"
   
   Then: "Great. Here's what I'll do:
   • By Friday, I'll find 200-300 people who match this profile
   • I'll write 5 different email versions
   • We'll send the first batch next week
   • You'll hear from me every Friday with results"

4. Set expectations (3 min)
   "Here's what to expect:
   • Some emails will go to spam (normal)
   • Open rates should be 30-40%
   • Reply rate should be 5-8%
   • Most replies will be from people asking questions
   • We'll follow up with a call pitch
   • Some will say yes to a meeting
   
   First week: We'll probably book 1-2 meetings
   By week 4: Should have 8+ total
   
   Make sense?"

HANDLING OBJECTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OBJECTION #1: "We don't know if cold email works for us"

RESPONSE:
"That's fair. That's exactly why we have the guarantee. We don't ask you to 
believe anything. We just ask you to let us try for 60 days. If it doesn't 
work, you don't pay. So there's zero risk."

OBJECTION #2: "We're already doing outbound"

RESPONSE:
"Nice. What are you doing?"
[Listen]
"And how many meetings is that generating per month?"
[Listen]
"And how's that working?"
[Listen]
"Gotcha. Most agencies we work with were doing something similar. The 
difference is we handle the entire process, so they can focus on closing deals. 
Plus, our personalization typically gets 2-3x better response rates. Want to 
try it?"

OBJECTION #3: "Your price is too high"

RESPONSE:
"I get it. $1,500 isn't cheap. But think about it this way: if we book you 
8-15 meetings per month, and you close even 1 or 2, what's the value of that?
[Wait for answer]
Most agencies tell me 1 client is worth $10-50K+ in revenue. So our fee pays 
for itself on the first deal. Plus, if we don't deliver, it's free. So really, 
there's no risk."

OBJECTION #4: "We need to think about it"

RESPONSE:
"I totally understand. Take 24 hours. But be honest with me - is this something 
you genuinely want to do? Or are you passing?
[If passing] No problem. If that changes, let me know.
[If genuine interest] Perfect. Take the time. But I'd love to get you started 
next week so we can hit the 60-day guarantee window. Sound good?"

OBJECTION #5: "Can you work with our CRM?"

RESPONSE:
"Yes, no problem. We can sync meetings to your calendar, and you can add them 
to your CRM yourself. Or if you want, I can show you how to set up the 
integration. Most agencies do it themselves in 10 minutes."
