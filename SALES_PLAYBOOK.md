**Your Complete Guide to Turning Leads into Customers**

**Goal:** 25-35% close rate from discovery calls

## PART 1: DISCOVERY CALL MASTERY

### The 20-Minute Discovery Call Structure

```
Opening:          2 min (build rapport, set agenda)
Discovery:        8 min (ask questions, listen)
Pitch:             5 min (show solution)
Close attempt:     3 min (ask for commitment)
Confirm:           2 min (next steps)

Total:            20 min
```

---

## Opening (2 Minutes)

### Script

```
"Hi [First Name], thanks for taking 20 minutes to chat. 

Before I dive in, I want to understand your situation first.

Is this still a good time?"

[Wait for yes]

"Great. So quick agenda for us:

First 10 minutes, I want to understand what you're dealing with.

Then, I'll show you how we typically help companies like yours.

Sound good?"

[Wait for yes]

"Perfect. Let's get started."
```

---

### What You're Doing

- ✅ Establishing rapport (friendly, not corporate)
- ✅ Setting permission (asking if they have time)
- ✅ Managing expectations (what will happen)
- ✅ Getting buy-in (they agree to process)

## Discovery (8 Minutes)

### Question 1: The Opener (0-2 min)

**Script:**
```
"When I looked at [Company Name], I noticed [specific fact: recently hired VP Sales / 
launched new product / got funding].

Quick question: What's your biggest challenge right now when it comes to sales pipeline?"

[LISTEN]
```

**Your Job:** Listen for 2 minutes without interrupting.

**What You're Listening For:**
- Consistency issue ("We have 15 one month, 5 the next")
- Volume problem ("We need 3x more pipeline")
- Quality issue ("We get leads but they don't close")
- Team issue ("Our SDRs aren't performing")

**Next Step:** Pick ONE challenge they mention. Ask about it specifically.

---

### Question 2: Dig Deeper (2-4 min)

**If They Said "Inconsistent Pipeline":**
```
"Help me understand a few things:

1) Right now, how many qualified leads are you generating per month?

[Wait for answer]

2) What does your sales cycle look like? 30 days? 60? 90?

[Wait for answer]

3) Of those leads, how much is from manual outreach vs automated?

[Wait for answer]

And quick question: If you could snap your fingers and 3x your pipeline, 
with no additional headcount required, would that move the needle for you?"

[Wait for yes/no]
```

**Your Job:** Take notes. Look for:
- Current lead volume (e.g., "20/month")
- Sales cycle length (e.g., "90 days")
- Manual vs automated mix (e.g., "95% manual, 5% automated")
- Their priority level (yes/no to 3x question)

---

**If They Said "Low Conversion Rate":**
```
"Interesting. So leads aren't the issue, conversion is.

A few questions:

1) What's your current close rate?

[Wait for answer]

2) What's the main objection you hear?

[Wait for answer]

3) Do you think it's a messaging problem, timing problem, or wrong ICP problem?

[Wait for answer]

4) If we could improve your close rate from X% to X+15%, what would that mean 
for your business?"

[Wait for answer]
```

**Your Job:** Understand if it's:
- Wrong messaging (not resonating with prospects)
- Timing (reaching them too late in buying cycle)
- ICP (talking to wrong people)
- Sales process (how they sell, not who they sell to)

---

**If They Said "Expensive to Acquire":**
```
"So cost is the issue. Let me understand:

1) What's your current cost per lead?

[Wait for answer]

2) How are you currently generating leads? (hiring SDRs, buying lists, ads, etc)

[Wait for answer]

3) What would an acceptable cost per lead look like?

[Wait for answer]

4) If we could get you to [their target cost] per lead, would you move on it?"

[Wait for yes/no]
```

**Your Job:** Understand their economics and pain threshold.

---

### Question 3: Qualification (4-6 min)

**Script:**
```
"Based on what you've told me, here's what I'm thinking:

We typically work with companies that:

1) Have a clear ICP - they know who they want to sell to
2) Have sales bandwidth - they can actually close deals 
3) Have good deal size - usually ₹15L+ to make the economics work

How does [Company Name] stack up on those three?

[Wait for response]

If all three are true, I think we could probably help you.

Here's what that would look like:

[TRANSITION TO PITCH]
```

**Your Job:** Quickly qualify if they fit your ideal customer profile.

**If ANY of these are false:**
- No clear ICP → Offer ICP workshop as first step
- No sales bandwidth → It's a sales process issue, not lead issue
- Small deal size → Wrong fit, politely decline

**If ALL three are true:** Move to pitch.

## Pitch (5 Minutes)

### The Problem → Solution → Results Framework

**Script:**
```
"Okay, so here's what I'm seeing:

You've got a pipeline problem. And you're trying to solve it the hard way:
- Hiring more SDRs (expensive, slow ramp time)
- Buying leads (low quality)
- Doing manual outreach (doesn't scale)

Here's what we do differently:

We take the three things that are killing your time and consistency:
1) Finding the right prospects
2) Personalizing outreach at scale
3) Managing follow-ups

And we automate ALL of it.

The result?

You get a consistent pipeline without any additional work from your team.

On average, our clients see:
- 3x more qualified leads (within 90 days)
- 40% improvement in close rate (because leads are higher quality)
- ₹50K-150K new MRR added

For [Company Name], specifically, I'd estimate:

Based on your [lead volume] and [sales cycle], we'd generate about [X] 
qualified leads per month for you.

At your [close rate], that's [Y] new deals per month.

At your average deal size of [Z], that's ₹[new MRR] new MRR.

Cost to you: ₹29,000/month.

Payback: About [X] months.

Make sense?"

[WAIT FOR RESPONSE]
```

---

### Key Elements of Good Pitch

✅ **Start with problem** (frame it as their problem, not your solution)  
✅ **Show contrast** (old way vs new way)  
✅ **Make it specific** (use their numbers, not generic stats)  
✅ **Show results** (real case studies, real metrics)  
✅ **Do the math** (show ROI calculation for THEM)  
✅ **End with question** (get them thinking, not pitching)

## PART 2: OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection 1: "Your Solution Sounds Great, But We're Doing Outbound Already"

**Root Cause:** They don't see difference between what they're doing and what you do.

**Handler:**
```
"That's great that you're already doing outbound. Quick question:

When you say you're doing outbound, are you:

A) Manually researching prospects and sending templated emails?
B) Using a tool like Lemlist or Instantly for personalization, but still 
   manually finding prospects?
C) Fully automated system where you find, personalize, send, and track all 
   automatically?

[Listen to answer]

If it's A or B, we can probably help you 3-5x your volume and quality.

If it's C, you're ahead of 99% of companies. Nice work.

Which one is it?"

[Listen and respond accordingly]
```

---

### Objection 2: "We're Concerned This Will Feel Spammy to Our Prospects"

**Root Cause:** They worry about brand damage or low-quality leads.

**Handler:**
```
"That's a legitimate concern. Most companies worry about that.

Here's the difference:

Spam = Mass emails to anyone with an email address.
Our approach = Highly personalized emails to prospects that fit your ICP.

We're not sending 10,000 emails to random people.

We're sending 100 personalized emails to 100 hand-picked prospects that 
actually fit your target.

Each email references specific research about their company.

It feels personal. Because it IS personal.

Plus, we only send to companies that are a fit. So the reply rate is 4-6%, 
not 0.1%.

That's the opposite of spam. That's targeted, relevant outreach.

Make sense?"
```

---

That's our guarantee.

## PART 4: POST-CLOSE ONBOARDING

### Week 1: ICP Workshop

**Objective:** Define ideal customer profile crystal clear

**Agenda:**
1. Company overview (10 min)
2. Best customers analysis (15 min)
3. Lost deals analysis (10 min)
4. ICP framework definition (15 min)
5. Action items + next steps (10 min)

**Output:** 1-page ICP document with:
- Company size range (employees)
- Revenue range
- Geographic focus
- Industry/vertical
- Growth stage
- Key pain points
- Buying signals
- Decision maker titles

---

### Week 2: List Building & Sequence Customization

**Objective:** Find first prospects + ready sequences

**Your Actions:**
- [ ] Build prospect list (500+)
- [ ] Customize 3 sequences for their market

- [ ] Review prospect list quality

### Churn Prevention

**Red Flags to Watch For:**
🚩 No engagement on emails (0% open rate)  
🚩 Can't book meetings (no replies)  
🚩 Can't close deals ("We have leads but no revenue")  
🚩 Slow sales team (not following up)  
🚩 Wrong ICP (leads not qualified)  

**Response to Each:**
- No engagement → Fix email copy + list quality
- No bookings → Add urgency + better CTA
- No closes → Review sales process, offer sales coaching
- Slow team → Set expectations on follow-up, daily check-ins
- Wrong ICP → Re-run ICP workshop, rebuild prospect list

## PART 6: KEY METRICS TO MONITOR

### During Sales Call

```
☐ Are they asking specific questions? (good sign)
☐ Are they picturing themselves using this? (good sign)
☐ Are they asking about ROI/payback? (good sign)
☐ Are they asking about guarantees? (good sign, risk-conscious)
☐ Are they checking with someone else? (neutral, expected)
☐ Are they pushing back on price? (neutral, shows they care about value)
```

---

### After Sales Call

```
☐ Did they say "yes" → Move to proposal
☐ Did they say "let me think" → Send resources + follow-up call next day
☐ Did they say "need to check with team" → Create one-pager, offer team call
☐ Did they say "revisit later" → Calendar reminder, send resources
☐ Did they say "no" → Ask why, see if there's a fit later
```

---

### Proposal Sent

```
☐ Track: How long until they read? (same day is good)
☐ Track: Do they ask questions? (good sign)
☐ Track: Do they ask for changes? (they're negotiating = interested)
☐ Track: How long until they decide? (2-3 days is good, 1 week is slow)
```
